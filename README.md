Design Idea: Minimal paper-like aesthetic, daily prompt cards, mood tagging system, calendar journal view, yearly reflection summary page.

Project Description:

A platform where users maintain a daily journal, reflect on entries, and track their emotional and personal growth.

 Problem Statement:

Many people struggle to maintain a consistent journaling habit due to blank-page intimidation, cluttered digital interfaces, and the lack of meaningful reflection tools. Existing digital note apps often feel too sterile and feature-heavy. At the same time, traditional physical notebooks make it difficult to organise past entries, tag emotional states, or track personal growth over time. Papertrail solves this by providing a distraction-free, paper-like writing environment equipped with daily prompt cards, mood tagging, a calendar view, and annual reflection summaries that make daily self-reflection engaging, intuitive, and insightful.
